Yes. All gold is stored in LBMA-certified vaults in London, Zurich, and Singapore. Your holdings are fully allocated, segregated, and insured up to $500M.
Can I take physical delivery of my gold? expand_more
Yes. You can request physical delivery of your gold holdings. We offer insured door-to-door shipping worldwide for gold bars (min 100g) and coins.
What are GIB Tokens? expand_more
AM (Avalanch Metal) Tokens are digital representations of physical gold. Each token is backed 1:1 by real gold in our vaults, allowing you to trade 24/7.
