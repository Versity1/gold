Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start investing in gold with Avalanch Metal? expand_more
Simply create a free account, complete your KYC verification, and deposit funds into your wallet. You can then purchase gold at live market prices or choose one of our managed investment plans. The entire process takes less than 10 minutes.
Is my gold physically stored and insured? expand_more
Yes. Every gram of gold you purchase is physically allocated and stored in LBMA-certified vaults in London, Zurich, or Singapore. Your holdings are fully insured by Lloyd's of London up to $500 million and are independently audited every quarter.
How quickly can I withdraw my funds? expand_more
You can request a withdrawal at any time. Standard processing takes 24–48 hours for bank transfers. Your gold can be liquidated instantly at live market prices with zero hidden fees.
What is the minimum investment amount? expand_more
You can start investing with as little as $50. We offer fractional gold ownership from 1 gram, making precious metal investment accessible to everyone regardless of budget.
