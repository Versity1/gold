Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I start investing in gold? expand_more
Create an account, complete identity verification, and fund your account. You can begin buying physical gold or AM tokens with as little as $50.
Is my gold stored in physical vaults? expand_more
Yes. All gold is stored in LBMA-certified vaults in London, Zurich, and Singapore. Your holdings are fully allocated, segregated, and insured up to $500M.
Can I take physical delivery of my gold? expand_more
Yes. You can request physical delivery of your gold holdings. We offer insured door-to-door shipping worldwide for gold bars (min 100g) and coins.
What are AM Tokens? expand_more
AM (Avalanch Metal) Tokens are digital representations of physical gold. Each token is backed 1:1 by real gold in our vaults, allowing you to trade 24/7.
What are the fees for investing? expand_more
We charge a transparent 0.5% transaction fee on buys and sells. Storage is free for the first year, then just 0.12% annually. No hidden fees, ever.
How is my investment secured? expand_more
Your gold is fully insured up to $500M by Lloyd's of London. We use bank-grade encryption, 2FA authentication, and cold storage for digital assets. All holdings are audited quarterly by independent auditors.
