Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start investing in gold? expand_more
Getting started is simple. Create an account, complete identity verification, and fund your account. You can begin buying physical gold or GIB tokens with as little as $100.
Is my gold really stored in physical vaults? expand_more
Yes. All gold is stored in LBMA-certified vaults in London, Zurich, and Singapore. Your holdings are fully allocated, segregated, and insured. You can request a vault audit report at any time.
What are GIB Tokens? expand_more
GIB (Gold Investment Broker) Tokens are digital representations of physical gold. Each token is backed 1:1 by real gold in our vaults, allowing you to trade gold 24/7 with instant settlement.
Can I take physical delivery of my gold? expand_more
Yes. You can request physical delivery of your gold holdings. We offer insured shipping worldwide for gold bars (minimum 100g) and coins. Delivery typically takes 5-10 business days.
What are the storage fees? expand_more
Storage fees are 0.12% annually, calculated daily and deducted monthly. This includes full insurance, vault security, and regular auditing. There are no hidden fees.
What payment methods do you accept? expand_more
We accept bank wire transfers, credit/debit cards, and cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT, and USDC.
How do I contact customer support? expand_more
Our support team is available 24/7 via live chat, email (support@goldinvestmentbroker.com), and phone. Premium account holders have access to dedicated account managers.
